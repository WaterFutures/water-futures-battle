## Third-party sources

This dataset contains data derived from the following sources, modified by the authors through the processing documented in the BWF repository (https://github.com/<TODO>):

- Statistics Netherlands (CBS), tables 70739ned and 70072ned and published population forecasts, CC BY 4.0.
- Eurostat, datasets nrg_pc_205 and nrg_bal_c, CC BY 4.0.
- European Environment Agency, greenhouse gas emissions data, CC BY 4.0.
- World Bank Open Data (International Monetary Fund, International Financial Statistics), CC BY 4.0.
- Our World in Data, solar PV module prices (original sources as cited on the chart page), CC BY 4.0.
- Royal Netherlands Meteorological Institute (KNMI), climate dashboard, CC0 1.0.
- Municipality coordinates © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0, via Nominatim.
- Statistical values from Vewin, Dutch Drinking Water Statistics 2022 (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0); the report itself is not included.
