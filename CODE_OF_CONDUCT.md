# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and collaborative community. This applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other interactions related to the Battle of the Water Futures.

## Expected Behaviour

- Communicate constructively and respectfully.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and provide helpful, evidence-based feedback.
- Respect privacy, attribution requirements, and data-use restrictions.
- Focus discussion on the project and its shared goals.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to [battlewaterfutures@kwrwater.nl](mailto:battlewaterfutures@kwrwater.nl). Reports will be handled promptly and as confidentially as practical. Project maintainers may remove content or take other appropriate action in response to violations.
